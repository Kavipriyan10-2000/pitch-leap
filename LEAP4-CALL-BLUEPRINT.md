## 7. Questions LEAP4 Will Likely Ask — With Prepared Answers

**"Why hasn't this been built already?"**
> Real-time multi-modal orchestration requires three things that only recently converged: accessible transit APIs, production-grade LLM agents (LangGraph), and sub-second voice pipelines. We're not early to the problem — we're on time for the infrastructure.

**"What's the moat? Can Google or Amadeus copy this?"**
> Google Maps handles routing, not disruption resolution. Amadeus handles booking, not live re-planning. Our moat is the stateful context graph — we track 8 live signals simultaneously and checkpoint agent state in Postgres. That architecture takes 18–24 months to replicate from scratch, and incumbents are built on static query systems that cannot be retrofitted.

**"What's your go-to-market? How do you acquire users?"**
> Phase 1: Direct B2C — tech professionals and frequent multi-modal travellers in the UK/DACH corridor. Phase 2: White-label enterprise pilots with corporate travel managers. Phase 3: API licensing to OEMs and transit operators — the LEAP4 LP network is exactly the right channel for that door.

**"Is the MVP live?"**
> The voice pipeline and orchestration engine are in finishing stage — launching mid-September 2026. We're not pre-product; we're pre-launch. The core loop is built and tested.

**"Why £1M? Why £8M valuation?"**
> £1M covers 18 months of runway: 60% engineering (latency and agent scaling), 25% growth (European user acquisition), 15% operations (GDPR, compliance). £8M is benchmarked against comparable pre-seed AI infrastructure rounds in Europe. We're not raising on revenue — we're raising on infrastructure defensibility and a clear path to £1.2M ARR.

**"What do you need from us beyond capital?"**
> Introductions to OEM mobility teams, transit operators, and corporate travel platforms — specifically where LEAP4's LP network overlaps with fleet and travel procurement. Dr. Matthias Kempf's relationships with VW Group, BMW, and Deutsche Bahn-adjacent operators are directly relevant.

**"How does Bevoyager.ai differ from what Hopper or TripActions already does?"**
> Hopper predicts prices. TripActions manages expenses after the fact. Neither resolves a live disruption in real time across multiple modes. We're the layer that runs during the trip, not before it or after it.
